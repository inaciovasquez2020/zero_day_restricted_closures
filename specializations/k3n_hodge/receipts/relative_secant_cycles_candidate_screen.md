## Relevance to the K3^[n] blocker

This is not a usable noncircular defect-witness construction and does not establish a concrete class outside (SH(X,\mathbb Q)). Its stated route is instead to assert a much stronger general result: the full Hodge conjecture for all hyperkähler varieties and, ultimately, all smooth complex projective varieties.

## Mathematical screening result

The cited LLV/Verbitsky results concern the cohomology algebra generated by (H^2) and/or the LLV Lie-algebra representation on total cohomology. They do not imply that every rational Hodge class is generated by divisor classes, nor that all Hodge classes lie in (SH(X,\mathbb Q)). Hard Lefschetz does not convert an arbitrary Hodge class into a polynomial in divisor classes. In addition, (H^2(X,\mathbb Q)) is not itself comprised entirely of divisor classes: only its rational ((1,1))-classes are divisor classes by Lefschetz ((1,1)).

Thus the inference at the cited step is invalid as stated. The paper's theorem cannot be used as an established external input for the required-class inventory or degree-four quotient obstruction. This screen is a logical assessment of the cited argument, not a peer review of every claim in the manuscript.
